# 🌟 Our Pledge

We are committed to creating a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for everyone.

Every contributor deserves to be treated with:

* 🤝 Respect
* 💙 Kindness
* 🌍 Inclusion
* 📚 Support
* 🚀 Equal Opportunity

No matter your background, experience, identity, or skill level, **you are welcome here.**

---

# ✅ Our Community Standards

We encourage everyone to:

* 💬 Be respectful in discussions
* 🤝 Help fellow contributors
* 💡 Share ideas openly
* 🧠 Accept constructive feedback
* ❤️ Learn from mistakes
* 🌱 Encourage new contributors
* 🎯 Focus on improving the project together

---

# 🚫 Unacceptable Behavior

To keep our community safe, the following are not permitted:

* Harassment or discrimination
* Personal attacks or insults
* Hate speech
* Bullying or intimidation
* Publishing private information without permission
* Offensive or inappropriate language
* Disruptive or toxic behavior

---

# 🛡️ Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for ensuring that this Code of Conduct is followed.

If a violation occurs, maintainers may:

* Issue a friendly reminder
* Provide a formal warning
* Temporarily restrict participation
* Permanently remove access in severe or repeated cases

All reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially.

---

# 📢 Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers through the repository's communication channels.

We take every report seriously and will handle it respectfully and confidentially.

---

# ⚖️ Community Impact Guidelines

Violations are generally addressed in four stages:

### 🟢 1. Correction

A private reminder explaining the issue and how to improve.

### 🟡 2. Warning

A formal warning with clear expectations for future behavior.

### 🟠 3. Temporary Ban

Temporary removal from community participation.

### 🔴 4. Permanent Ban

Permanent removal for severe or repeated violations.
